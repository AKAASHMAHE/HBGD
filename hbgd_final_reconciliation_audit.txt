5. RESOLUTION OF DISCREPANCY (7.5275 mm vs 7.5301 mm vs 7.4492 mm)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Investigation of the three reported mean MAE numbers for `Domain_Adapted_HBGD`:

1. **7.5301 mm**: **Fold-Level Average MAE** — The exact arithmetic mean of the 25 fold-level validation MAEs (`step27_fold_level_metrics.csv`). This is the standard machine learning cross-validation metric.
2. **7.5275 mm**: **Patient-Level Average MAE** — The mean across the 54 unique development patients' individual mean absolute errors (`step27_patient_level_performance.csv`). Patient-level weighting accounts for unequal observation counts per patient.
3. **7.4492 mm**: **Pooled Case-Level MAE** — The unweighted mean across all 1605 prediction rows in `step27_cross_validation_predictions.csv`.

Resolution: All three numbers are mathematically correct under their respective aggregation levels. The primary machine learning cross-validation benchmark is **7.5301 mm (Fold-Level Mean)**.


6. AUDIT OF STATISTICAL UNCERTAINTY & PAIRED SIGNIFICANCE TESTS
---------------------------------------------------------------
Methodology Audit:
- Standard deviations and 95% confidence intervals in `step27_aggregated_model_comparison.csv` are calculated across fold-level validation MAEs (N = 25 folds), reflecting fold-to-fold split sampling variation.
- Paired statistical significance tests were conducted across the 54 development patients (`step27_paired_differences.csv`):
  - Domain-Adapted HBGD Mean Patient MAE: 8.0807 mm
  - Target Morphology Baseline Mean Patient MAE: 8.2500 mm
  - Mean Patient-Level MAE Reduction: +0.1693 mm
  - Patients Improved: 33 / 54 (61.11%)
  - Patients Equal or Worse: 21 / 54 (38.89%)
  - **Paired t-test**: t = 1.2166, p-value = **0.2291** (p > 0.05)
  - **Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test**: W = 594.0, p-value = **0.2010** (p > 0.05)

Conclusion on Uncertainty:
The 95% confidence intervals overlap substantially ([5.7375, 10.1778] mm vs [5.9098, 10.3963] mm), and neither the paired t-test nor Wilcoxon test reaches two-tailed statistical significance ($\alpha = 0.05$). The improvement is **directionally positive but statistically unestablished**.


7. INVESTIGATION OF DIRECT HISTORICAL-RATE MODEL INSTABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------
Tracing the Direct HBGD Historical Rate Model (Mean CV MAE = 260.5074 mm vs Median = 14.7544 mm):

- Cause Identified: Unbounded exponential rate extrapolation over long prediction horizons.
- Specific Extreme Outlier Case:
  - **Patient ID**: Patient-043 (in repeat_1_fold_2, repeat_2_fold_3, repeat_3_fold_4, repeat_4_fold_1, repeat_5_fold_2).
  - **Trajectory**: Between Week 0 ($D_1 = 15.08 \text{ mm}$) and Week 2 ($D_2 = 23.02 \text{ mm}$), Patient-043 exhibited transient rapid growth, producing a rate $r = 0.030214 \text{ day}^{-1}$.
  - **Prediction Horizon**: Week 2 to Week 41 ($\Delta t = 39 \text{ weeks} = 273 \text{ days}$).
  - **Unbounded Direct Prediction**: $D_{\text{pred}} = 23.02 \times \exp(0.030214 \times 273) = 86,233.09 \text{ mm}$ (86 meters!).
  - **Actual Target Diameter**: $3.65 \text{ mm}$.
  - **Resulting Prediction Error**: $86,229.44 \text{ mm}$.

Machine Learning Mitigation:
Random Forest regressors in Domain-Adapted HBGD and Target Baseline bound output predictions within tree leaf ranges, naturally preventing exponential explosion.
