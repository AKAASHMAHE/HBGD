1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------
This project investigates whether tumor growth dynamics learned from source cancer longitudinal growth trajectories (RECIST dataset: 74 observation intervals across 25 trajectories / 12 unique patients) can be transferred to predict future tumor diameters in a target cancer cohort (LUMIERE Glioblastoma dataset: 476 longitudinal imaging observations across 67 unique patients), and whether target-domain residual adaptation improves predictive precision. The primary objective of this audit is to conduct an independent, scientifically defensible, leak-free, and reproducible evaluation suitable for research documentation and preparation of an Invention Disclosure Form (IDF-B).

3. ORIGINAL IMPLEMENTATION AND DATA FLOW
---------------------------------------
Data Flow Pipeline:
1. Raw Source Trajectories (DATA/17_hbgd_diameter_trajectories.csv) -> Preprocessed into ordered timepoints per (patient, lesion) -> Historical growth rate features (recent_rate, mean_rate, std_rate, prior) constructed strictly from past timepoints -> Random Forest Source Model fitted on 49 next-timepoint source prediction cases from 12 patients.
2. Raw Target Trajectories (DATA/LUMIRE/lumiere_longitudinal_trajectories.csv) -> Preprocessed into ordered timepoints per patient -> Week multiplied by 7.0 to convert to elapsed days -> 67 target patients split into train (47 patients, 255 prediction cases), validation (7 patients, 66 prediction cases), and test (13 patients, 88 prediction cases).
3. Inference & Adaptation -> Source model predicts on target cases using source feature schema. Residuals (actual - source_pred) calculated on target train cases -> Residual adaptation Random Forest fitted on target train cases -> Predictions generated for validation and test patients for No_Change, Direct_HBGD_Rate, Source_HBGD_Transfer, Target_Morphology_History_Baseline, Domain_Adapted_HBGD, and Morphology_Plus_Adapted_HBGD models.

5. CONFIRMED ISSUES
-------------------
1. Over-optimistic Single-Split Interpretation (CONFIRMED): Relying on a single 13-patient test split produces noisy comparative metrics. In Step 11 test set, Target Baseline (7.8918 mm) outperforms Domain Adapted HBGD (7.9370 mm) by -0.57% and Combined Model (7.9691 mm) by -0.98%.
2. Absence of Patient-Grouped Cross-Validation on Development Cohort (CONFIRMED): The baseline script did not evaluate repeated patient-grouped cross-validation on the 54 development patients prior to reporting performance.
3. Conflation of Predictive Transfer with Biological Parameter Invariance (CONFIRMED): The literature and project notes previously risked confusing ML residual regression transfer with direct biological parameter invariance ($r$ or $k$).
4. Sensitivity of Exponential Direct Rate Extrapolation (CONFIRMED): Direct HBGD historical rate extrapolation without bounding or ML smoothing produces extreme errors (Test MAE 15.8693 mm, CV MAE > 200 mm) due to exponential explosion over multi-week prediction horizons.


6. METHODOLOGICAL RISKS AND LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------
1. Small Source Sample Size: The source model relies on 49 prediction cases from 12 patients.
2. Target Patient Heterogeneity: LUMIERE glioblastoma trajectories exhibit high variance in growth rates post-resection/radiotherapy.
3. Week-to-Day Constant Assumption: Assuming exactly 7.0 days per week is standard in clinical datasets but subject to minor scan schedule jitter (+/- 2 days).
4. Short Longitudinal Horizons: Most patients have 3 to 6 imaging timepoints, limiting high-order historical rate variance estimation.

14. PATIENT-LEVEL PERFORMANCE AND UNCERTAINTY
---------------------------------------------
- Distribution of Paired Differences (Domain Adapted HBGD vs Target Morphology Baseline):
  - Out of 54 development patients, Domain Adapted HBGD improved prediction in 31 patients (57.4%), while Target Baseline was equal or better in 23 patients (42.6%).
  - Mean per-patient MAE reduction: +0.1706 mm.
  - 95% Confidence Interval of Mean CV MAE for Domain Adapted HBGD: [5.7396, 10.1679] mm.
  - 95% Confidence Interval for Target Baseline: [5.9098, 10.3963] mm.
- Key takeaway: The 95% confidence intervals overlap substantially, confirming that domain adaptation provides a modest directional benefit but is not statistically significant.


15. BIOLOGICAL PARAMETER TRANSFER ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------
- Biological Growth Parameter ($r$) Analysis:
  - Estimated source cohort mean growth rate $r_{source} = 0.00341 \text{ day}^{-1}$ ($\text{std} = 0.00482 \text{ day}^{-1}$).
  - When applied directly to predict target glioblastoma growth without target outcome calibration:
    - Direct Biological Parameter Transfer Mean CV MAE = 9.7200 mm.
    - No-Change Baseline Mean CV MAE = 8.2307 mm.
    - Target Baseline Mean CV MAE = 7.6981 mm.
- Scientific Conclusion: Direct biological parameter transfer without target domain adaptation performs significantly WORSE than even the no-change baseline (+1.4893 mm higher error). This conclusively disproves the claim of direct biological parameter invariance across RECIST source cancers and LUMIERE glioblastoma. Predictive transfer is achieved strictly via machine-learning residual domain adaptation, NOT direct biological parameter invariance.
