HBGD EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION REPORT
====================================================================================================



1. MATHEMATICAL CORE

The project successfully implements an HBGD-style dynamical formulation:

    dD/dt = rD

with solution:

    D(t) = D0 * exp(r * t)

The experiments establish that this mathematical formulation
can be implemented on the available longitudinal lesion data.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. FORWARD PREDICTION

Step 18 demonstrated genuine forward prediction in which the
initial observation was used to estimate the initial rate and
later observations were treated as prediction targets.

The HBGD formulation substantially outperformed the no-change
baseline in that exploratory experiment.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. PATIENT-INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE

Step 19 evaluated population parameters on held-out patients.

The state-aware HBGD formulation outperformed the global
exponential model and the no-change baseline in that experiment.

This provides preliminary evidence that the mathematical
architecture contains useful predictive structure.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. INDIVIDUALIZED PARAMETER

Step 21 showed that an individualized growth parameter derived
from the initial transition can outperform fixed population
parameters in the available held-out experiment.

This supports the architectural hypothesis that the dynamical
parameter may be lesion-specific rather than universally fixed.

------------------------------------------------------------

5. CT INPUT PIPELINE

Steps 24-26 established that RECIST lesions can be linked to
segmentation labels and lesion-specific CT crops can be extracted.

44 physical CT crops were successfully available.

12 longitudinal CT trajectories were recovered.

------------------------------------------------------------

6. CT TO RATE PROTOTYPE

Step 33 implemented:

    CT crop
        |
        v
    3D CNN
        |
        v
    predicted r
        |
        v
    HBGD equation
        |
        v
    predicted future diameter

The complete pipeline executed successfully.

However, the independent test set contained only one usable
trajectory.

Therefore the CNN result cannot be considered statistical,
clinical, or generalizable validation.

------------------------------------------------------------

7. CURRENT LIMITATION

The major remaining limitation is NOT the HBGD mathematical
implementation.

The major limitation is longitudinal CT coverage in independent
patients.

The available data do not provide enough independent CT
trajectories for a defensible CNN validation experiment.

------------------------------------------------------------

8. WHAT IS DEMONSTRATED

The current project demonstrates a working research prototype
of the following architecture:

    longitudinal lesion measurements
        |
        v
    individualized dynamical parameter
        |
        v
    HBGD differential equation
        |
        v
    forward tumor-size prediction

and an exploratory imaging extension:

    lesion-specific CT
        |
        v
    3D CNN
        |
        v
    individualized r
        |
        v
    HBGD
        |
        v
    future diameter

------------------------------------------------------------

9. WHAT IS NOT DEMONSTRATED

The current experiments do NOT establish:

    - clinical validity
    - biological causality
    - universal cancer applicability
    - clinical superiority
    - regulatory readiness
    - clinical deployment readiness
    - statistically reliable CNN performance

A larger longitudinal CT dataset with independent patients
would be required for those claims.

------------------------------------------------------------

10. RECOMMENDED NEXT DEVELOPMENT

The current dataset should be treated as a feasibility and
prototype dataset.

The next major experiment should use a substantially larger
longitudinal CT dataset with:

    - multiple time points
    - lesion identity across time
    - segmentation masks
    - RECIST measurements
    - sufficient independent patients

Only then should the CT-to-r neural component be trained and
evaluated as a true predictive model.
